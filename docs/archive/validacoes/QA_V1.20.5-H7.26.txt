H7.26 CONTRACT
==============
- Research is a first-class perspective inside the Service Workspace and appears before Ecology/Journey.
- Research projects, source materials and field observations are stored canonically in ServiceGraph.research.
- Supported methods include interview, observation, Service Safari, shadowing, survey, support, analytics, document, workshop, experiment and other.
- Observation levels distinguish what people said, what was observed, what was measured and contextual material.
- Observation -> Evidence promotion preserves project/source/observation provenance.
- Insight creation requires supporting evidence IDs.
- Insights may promote to Need or Problem Hypothesis, but never directly to a validated Problem.
- Existing H7.6 validation rule remains authoritative: Problems require a validated hypothesis with evidence.
- Traceability is derived from the canonical graph instead of a parallel browser-only pipeline.
- Research Studio exposes Projects, Field & Materials, Evidence, Insights and Traceability.
- Service Workspace readiness now considers five perspectives: Research, Ecology, Journey, Blueprint and Whiteboard.
- Sidebar Pesquisa opens the unified Research Studio rather than the legacy research overlay.
