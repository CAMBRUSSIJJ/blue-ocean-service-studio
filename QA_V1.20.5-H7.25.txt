H7.25 CONTRACT
==============
- Service Ecology is a first-class perspective inside the Service Workspace.
- Ecology is stored canonically on Service; no parallel browser-only model is introduced.
- Stakeholders support customer, employee, manager, partner, supplier, regulator, community, organization, system and other roles.
- Stakeholders carry influence, interest, evidence references and optional Persona provenance.
- Personas can be synchronized into the ecology without losing traceability.
- Explicit semantic relations connect stakeholders: uses, delivers, depends on, pays, influences, regulates, supports, exchanges information and creates value.
- Removing a stakeholder cleans its ecology relations.
- Ecology Health is deterministic and highlights disconnected actors and evidence coverage.
- The UI includes a visual ecology map and an Influence × Interest stakeholder matrix.
- Service Workspace readiness now considers four perspectives: Ecology, Journey, Blueprint and Whiteboard.
- Journey, Blueprint and Whiteboard remain independent canonical artifacts.
